Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy covers maikotrindade.com. The Capitol Trades Tracker app has its own privacy policy.

What this site collects

This site has no accounts, comments or contact forms, so it does not ask you for personal information. Third-party services collect some data automatically when you visit, as described below.

Advertising

Some blog posts show ads served by Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites.
  • Google's advertising cookies let Google and its partners serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's My Ad Center, or opt out of some third-party vendors at aboutads.info.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent where it is required. Read more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how pages are used, such as which pages are visited and roughly where visitors come from. Google Analytics uses cookies for this. You can install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on to stop this collection.

Other services

  • Hosting and delivery: GitHub Pages and Cloudflare serve this site and may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and protect against abuse.
  • Fonts: Web fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser requests them.

Stored on your device

Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works without them.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@maikotrindade.com.
